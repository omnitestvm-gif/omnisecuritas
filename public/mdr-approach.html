MDR, EXPLAINED

The details
that matter.

What does your MDR service focus on?+

Our documented approach centers on endpoint activity monitoring, investigation of suspicious behavior, coordinated response, reporting, and recommendations. Your agreement defines the exact devices, technology, and service arrangements.

Will you replace our internal IT team?+

We work alongside your team. We agree on communication channels, responsibilities, escalation contacts, and response permissions so the managed service supports your operations.

Which platforms and operating systems are supported?+

Platform support depends on the technology selected and the agreed service scope. We review your device inventory and confirm compatibility during discovery before proposing deployment.

Do you take action automatically?+

Response actions depend on the platform, your permissions, and the service agreement. We define which actions can be taken and which need your approval before the service begins.

How are coverage and response times defined?+

Monitoring coverage, escalation procedures, and response commitments are agreed during scoping and recorded in your service agreement. Contact us to discuss the arrangements appropriate to your organization.

Can we add assessments or incident readiness services?+

Yes, we can discuss separate professional services engagements for assessments, penetration testing, incident readiness, response, or forensic investigation alongside your managed security requirements.
