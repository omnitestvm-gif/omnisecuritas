How is MDR different from antivirus?+

Antivirus helps block known malicious files and behavior. MDR adds a managed service: analysts review suspicious activity, investigate context, and work with your team on an appropriate response. These layers complement one another.

Can you work with our existing IT team?+

Yes. Our managed approach is designed to support your team. We discuss your environment, responsibilities, communication channels, and response approvals before agreeing on the scope of service.

Is this suitable for a growing business?+

Our published offering is designed for organizations of different sizes. We start by understanding your devices, risks, priorities, and resources, then discuss a suitable combination of solutions.

What happens during an initial consultation?+

We discuss your current environment, security concerns, and goals. From there, we can identify relevant services and outline next steps. Coverage, platform support, response procedures, and commercial terms are agreed during scoping.
