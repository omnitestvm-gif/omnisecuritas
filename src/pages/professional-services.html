How do you define an engagement?+

We discuss your objectives, the environment involved, authorized activities, responsibilities, timing, and expected deliverables. The agreed scope determines the work.

Can you work alongside our existing providers?+

We can discuss collaboration with your internal team and relevant service providers. Access, responsibilities, communication, and any required permissions are agreed before work begins.

Can professional services complement MDR?+

Yes. An assessment or readiness engagement can support a wider security program alongside managed monitoring and response. Professional services are scoped separately to address specific objectives.

Can I submit incident evidence through the contact form?+

The contact form is for initial sales inquiries. Do not include credentials, sensitive evidence, or confidential incident details. If investigation support is agreed, appropriate communication and evidence-handling arrangements can be established.
